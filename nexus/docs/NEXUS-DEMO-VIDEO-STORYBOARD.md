### Universal Patterns (Present in 6+ of 8 companies):

1. **Sans-serif typography** -- Every single one uses sans-serif (Inter, custom fonts, system fonts)
2. **Voiceover carries narrative** -- The voice explains WHY, visuals show WHAT
3. **Under 2 minutes for hero** -- Nobody's hero/homepage video exceeds 2 minutes
4. **Single CTA at end** -- Simple, low-friction call to action
5. **Music never dominates** -- Always under the voiceover, rhythmic consistency
6. **Clean backgrounds** -- Either dark mode (Stripe/Vercel/Linear) or clean white (Notion/Figma)
7. **No talking heads in hero** -- Save those for testimonials and deeper content
8. **Feature benefits over feature names** -- "Never miss a message" not "Channel notification settings"

## PART 2: Demo Video Structures -- Which Works Best for Nexus?

### Structure 1: Problem-Solution-Demo-CTA (Classic)

**How it works:** Open with pain (manual work is killing you), introduce the solution (meet Nexus), demo the product (watch it work), close with CTA (try it free).

**Best for:** Products with a clear, universal pain point.
**Used by:** Asana, Monday.com, most B2B SaaS.
**Pros:** Proven framework. Easy to follow. Hits emotional triggers.
**Cons:** Can feel formulaic. Every SaaS does this.

### Structure 2: Day-in-the-Life

**How it works:** Follow a persona through their workday. Show them drowning in tasks, then show the same day with the product.

**Best for:** Productivity tools where the user IS the hero.
**Used by:** Slack (office scenarios), Notion (creative workflows).
**Pros:** Highly relatable. Builds empathy.
**Cons:** Requires lifestyle footage (expensive) or strong illustration.

### Structure 3: Feature Showcase (Rapid-Fire)

**How it works:** Quick cuts between features. "This. And this. And this too. All in one place."

**Best for:** Feature-rich products differentiating from competitors.
**Used by:** Monday.com (board views), Linear (keyboard shortcuts).
**Pros:** Shows breadth. Impressive if product is polished.
**Cons:** Can feel overwhelming. No emotional hook.

### Structure 4: Before/After (Manual vs. Automated)

**How it works:** Split-screen or sequential showing the OLD way (painful) vs the NEW way (magical).

**Best for:** Automation tools where the contrast is dramatic.
**Used by:** Zapier (chaos vs calm), automation-focused SaaS.
**Pros:** Instantly communicates value. Visceral impact.
**Cons:** Can feel gimmicky if not well executed.

### Structure 5: Testimonial-Driven

**How it works:** Real customers describe their problems and how the product solved them, interspersed with product shots.

**Best for:** Established products with recognizable customer logos.
**Used by:** Salesforce, HubSpot, enterprise SaaS.
**Pros:** Social proof is powerful.
**Cons:** Requires real customers. Not viable for a NEW product launch.

---

### RECOMMENDATION FOR NEXUS: Hybrid Structure -- "Magic Moment" Framework

**Structure: Hook-Pain-Magic-Proof-CTA**

This is a custom hybrid optimized for a NEW challenger entering the market against Zapier:

1. **HOOK** (0-3s) -- Bold statement that challenges the status quo
2. **PAIN** (3-13s) -- Show the manual workflow hell everyone knows (Before/After element)
3. **MAGIC MOMENT** (13-55s) -- User types plain English, Nexus builds the workflow LIVE (Problem-Solution-Demo fusion)
4. **PROOF** (55-65s) -- "500+ integrations, trusted globally, GCC-ready" (lite social proof without requiring testimonials)
5. **CTA** (65-75s) -- "Try Nexus free. Your workflows, in plain English."

**Why this wins for a new product:**
- Structure 5 (testimonials) is out -- no customers yet
- Pure Structure 3 (feature showcase) won't differentiate from Zapier's established brand
- The "Magic Moment" (typing plain English and watching a workflow appear) is Nexus's killer differentiator that no competitor can match
- Combines the emotional contrast of Structure 4 (before/after) with the demo credibility of Structure 1

### Scene 4: THE MAGIC MOMENT -- AI BUILDS THE WORKFLOW
**Duration:** 25 seconds
**Frame:** 00:20 - 00:45

**This is the centerpiece of the entire video. This scene must be flawless.**

**Visual Description:**
Full-screen view of the Nexus chat interface (actual product recording).

**Seconds 20-25:**
A cursor types into the chat input:
> "When I get an email with an attachment, save it to Dropbox and notify me on Slack."

The message sends. A brief thinking animation (the AI processing indicator).

**Seconds 25-32:**
Nexus responds. The response message appears, and then -- the MAGIC -- a `WorkflowPreviewCard` animates in below the text response. Three visual nodes appear one by one, connected by animated lines:

```
[Gmail icon] -----> [Dropbox icon] -----> [Slack icon]
  Trigger           Save File              Notify
```

Each node slides in with a satisfying micro-animation. Connection lines draw themselves between nodes. The card shows:
- Workflow name: "Email Attachment Auto-Save"
- Steps listed with app icons
- "Connect" buttons for each integration
- An "Execute Workflow" button at the bottom

**Seconds 32-38:**
Quick demonstration of the one-click connect flow:
- User clicks "Connect Gmail" -- a popup briefly appears (simulated)
- Green checkmark appears on Gmail node
- Same for Dropbox -- green checkmark
- Slack -- green checkmark
- All three connected. The "Execute Workflow" button lights up green.

**Seconds 38-45:**
User clicks "Execute." A progress animation runs through the nodes:
- Gmail node glows blue (executing)
- Arrow animates to Dropbox -- Dropbox glows blue
- Arrow animates to Slack -- Slack glows blue
- All three turn green (success)
- A small "Workflow Active" badge appears

Text overlay appears at bottom:
> "Built in seconds. Runs forever."

**Voiceover:**
"Type what you want. Watch it appear. 'When I get an email with an attachment, save it to Dropbox and notify me on Slack.' That's it. Nexus understands your intent, builds the workflow, and connects your apps -- one click each. Hit execute. Done. Built in seconds. Runs forever."

**Music/Mood:**
Building momentum through the typing. A satisfying "click" sound effect when each node appears. Warm, confident synth progression. A subtle "completion" chime when all nodes turn green.

**Shot Type:**
Screen recording of actual Nexus product (primary). This needs to be a real, working recording -- not a mockup.

**Transition:**
The workflow card gently zooms out, shrinking into the corner as the background expands to show more product features (Scene 5).

**$0 Toolkit:**
- Nexus dev server (`npm run dev`): Record the ACTUAL product working
- OBS: Screen record at 1080p, 30fps, crop to browser viewport
- DaVinci Resolve: Add cursor highlighting, zoom effects on key moments, sound effects for node appearances
- Audacity (free): Create/edit click and chime sound effects
- Tip: Pre-configure the workflow response in the AI to ensure a clean recording. Run multiple takes.

## APPENDIX D: Post-Production Workflow

**In DaVinci Resolve (free):**

1. **Import** all raw footage, voiceover audio, music track, Canva exports (as PNG/MP4)
2. **Edit Page** -- Assemble rough cut matching the storyboard timeline
3. **Sync** voiceover to visuals using the timing marks from each scene
4. **Fusion Page** -- Create:
   - Typewriter text effect (Scene 1)
   - Particle dissolve (Scene 1 transition)
   - Node appearance animations (if enhancing Scene 4)
   - Map with glow points (Scene 6)
   - Floating particle background (Scene 7)
5. **Color Page** -- Apply consistent color grade:
   - Lift shadows slightly for richness
   - Add subtle vignette
   - Ensure Nexus UI colors are accurate
6. **Fairlight Page** -- Audio mixing:
   - Voiceover: -6dB to -3dB (dominant)
   - Music: -18dB to -12dB (under VO)
   - Sound effects: -12dB to -6dB (accent)
7. **Deliver Page** -- Export:
   - YouTube: H.264, 1080p, 30fps, ~20Mbps
   - Website: H.264, 1080p, 30fps, ~10Mbps (smaller file)
   - Social: H.264, vertical crops as needed
